# Capturing screenshots & clips

The game is *engineered to clip* — near-misses, catches, and panic moments are the marketing. Here's how to grab them for the Steam page and socials.

## Screenshots (built in)

Press **`P`** during play (solo or multiplayer) — the current frame downloads as `human-error-<timestamp>.png`.

- The canvas is 960×600. Steam wants 1280×720 or larger, so upscale 2× in any editor, or run the page zoomed in (Ctrl + +) and use an OS screenshot of the canvas region for a crisper grab.
- Best shots: a Hunter mid-lock with the red cone, a catch kill-cam with the **HUMAN ERROR** stamp, a crowd packed wall-to-wall.

## GIFs / video clips (use a recorder)

Browser canvas → GIF is heavy and lossy; just screen-record instead. Pick one:

- **Windows:** `Win + Alt + R` (Xbox Game Bar) records the active window to MP4. Or **ShareX** (free) → record region → export GIF.
- **macOS:** `Cmd + Shift + 5` records a region to MP4.
- **Cross-platform:** [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com) for high-quality capture; **ScreenToGif** (Windows) for direct GIF.

### What clips well (aim for 6 seconds)
- A hider holding still as the Hunter's cone sweeps right past — then moves a frame too early and gets tagged.
- A dumb-human bot panic-sprinting across an idle crowd and getting popped.
- A glitch event (fire alarm) where everyone bolts and one human freezes.
- The catch **kill-cam** zoom + stamp — it's built to be the punchline; let it play out.

## Steam page asset sizes (for later)
- **Header capsule:** 460×215 · **Main capsule:** 616×353 · **Library hero:** 3840×1240
- **Screenshots:** 1920×1080 preferred · **Trailer:** 1920×1080, H.264 MP4

> Tip: each match rolls a random map + crowd skin, so a handful of recordings already gives visual variety for a trailer cut.
